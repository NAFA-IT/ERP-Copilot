# Summary (100 words)

ERP Copilot is an AI-powered assistant embedded in the procure-to-pay flow
that turns live purchase order, inventory, and vendor data into prioritized,
plain-language decisions. It continuously scores overdue POs, stockout risk,
and vendor performance, ranks the resulting issues by severity, and uses
Claude to generate a short daily briefing with concrete next actions — plus
answer ad-hoc questions grounded strictly in the live data. The prototype
reads from Google Sheets so any ERP export can feed it, and runs as a
Streamlit app. Target users are procurement and operations managers who need
faster, evidence-based decisions without manually reconciling three systems.
